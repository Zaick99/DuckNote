### Changed

- **Close, minimise and zoom sit in the title strip on the Mac**: centred in its height and aligned with the sidebar button, instead of hugging the top edge of the window. They stay there through resizing and after leaving full screen.
- **Full screen gives the space back**: when macOS hides the window buttons, the buttons at the top slide left to take their place, and slide back right when the window leaves full screen.
