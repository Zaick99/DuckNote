**A map you read at a glance, a Rete page built around what you do, and no more hosts called down by mistake.**

> The application's interface is in Italian.

### Added

- **The map opens to look at**, clean, with **Reti** (zones, VLANs and subnets; a click lights up one VLAN) and **Legenda** (only the cables the map uses) on the left; **Modifica mappa** (or `E`) opens library, tools and card, **Fine** goes back.
- **Every device is a card** with a tab for name and state (**UP**, **DOWN**, **?**), icon, model, address and what is known now; switches, routers and firewalls show their front panel with the ports lit by their cables; Internet is a cloud.
- **Commands in the card of a host** in **Rete**: **Ricontrolla**, **Apri** ▾ (web pages, shares, SSH on the Mac, Remote Desktop on Windows — only what its ports offer), **Promemoria**, and **⋯** with copy, **Mostra sulla mappa**, **Gruppi** ▸ and **Metti da parte**.
- **Groups edited in one click**: a pencil next to the chosen group, **Modifica un gruppo** ▸ in the menu, and **Gruppi** ▸ on a host to add it, take it out, or start a new group with it.
- **Animations**: quick page fades, the map arriving in a wave, the calendar day by day, the hosts of **Rete** in a cascade; *Reduce motion* turns them off.

### Changed

- **Cables at right angles**, around the cards, leaving a switch from their port: green when up, red dashed when down, VLAN colour for access ports, light and thick for trunks.
- **Rete bar**: **Analizza** becomes **Ferma** while scanning; **Controlla la nota**; one **Esporta** ▾ menu (table in the note, CSV file).
- **Notifications** in the shape of the map cards: a tab with what they are about and the time, a coloured line, the icon on its tile.
- Deleting a calendar item always asks first.

### Fixed

- **Hosts that were up no longer turn red during scans**: a missed ping is checked again calmly (more patience, and the open ports) before a host is called down; pings go out in steps, at most 256 at a time; a ping that fails on this computer leaves the host to check, not down.
- A host that goes down no longer shows its last response time, and a full scan no longer wipes the details of a host that missed a ping.
