**The map drawn like Vantage, with the real ports and the VPN links; devices recognised for what they are, and notifications without buttons.**

> The application's interface is in Italian.

### Added

- **VPN links show their service** — Tailscale, NetBird, NordVPN Meshnet, ZeroTier, Cloudflare, WireGuard, OpenVPN, IPsec — drawn in the main colour of its logo, with the logo on the link; the legend gets one row per service.
- **Ports on every device** (PCs, servers, NAS, access points, printers...), chosen with one click among the usual numbers of their kind or written, plus **SFP** cages; `eth0` is the first port on a device that counts from zero.
- **The card of a port**: resting on a port says its number and name, state and speed, the other end and its VLANs.
- **Verifica le porte degli host offline** in **Impostazioni** (on by default): a host that drops the ping is knocked on at the common ports before being called down; accepting or refusing the connection means it is up.
- **Riconosciuto da**, the model and the mDNS services in the card of a host.

### Changed

- **The map looks like Vantage, almost one to one**: tabs astride the top edge, coloured top borders, real front panels with RJ45 jacks, SFP cages, LEDs and the VLAN bars of every port, cables thick by speed with the speed by the end they reach; **Reti** and **Legenda** at the top right, zoom and **Modifica mappa** at the bottom left, the maps as tabs.
- **Devices are recognised from many clues**: time-to-live, the maker of the network card (the whole IEEE registry built in), typical ports (iPhone, Windows, Android, Google Cast, MikroTik, Home Assistant), names, mDNS (the model and macOS version of a Mac, Apple TV, HomePod, Chromecast, printers), SSH/HTTP/SNMP/UPnP banners and WMI.
- **Notifications have no buttons**: a click opens what they are about, **×** closes them; **Fatto** and **Rimanda** of a reminder are in its right-click menu.

### Fixed

- **On the Mac every host looked like Windows**: .NET reports there the time-to-live of the ping sent, not the one received; DuckNote now reads the real one.
- A Samba share or `xrdp` no longer makes a NAS, a Mac or a Linux server *Host Windows*.
