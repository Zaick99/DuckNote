**A calendar with reminders, and scans organised in groups.**

> The application's interface is in Italian.

### Added

- **Calendario**, a new page: appointments, things to do, deadlines and notes for the day, in a month from Monday, with the chosen day, what is late and the next 14 days. Items repeat (day, week, two weeks, month, three or six months, year), are dragged to another day, are searched; a deletion can be undone. Saved inside the note, encrypted with it.
- **Reminders while DuckNote is open**: a card that stays until it is answered — **Fatto**, **Rimanda** (10 minutes, an hour, tomorrow at 9), **Apri** — with the Dock icon bouncing on the Mac and the taskbar button flashing on Windows when DuckNote is behind. When the note opens, a summary of today, what is late and the certificates about to expire.
- **Certificate deadlines from the scans**: every TLS certificate a scan reads becomes a deadline of its host, with reminders 30 days, a week and a day before.
- **Items linked to hosts and map devices**, with their state; **Aggiungi un promemoria** in **Rete** and **Promemoria** on a device start one already linked.
- **Scan groups**: networks, ranges, addresses and names grouped next to **Nota**, from the new group menu of **Rete**, which filters the table and scans the chosen group. **Monitora** per group keeps its hosts checked like those of the note.
- **Groups on the map**: **Dalla rete** shows the hosts group by group, and **Aggiungi tutti** brings in those of the chosen group.

### Changed

- A range scan no longer drops the hosts of the groups; a device chosen from the calendar opens on its own map.
