**The Mac version answers the click again.** Buttons press and spring back, segmented controls slide their pill, and the app has a new icon and a disk image with a window of its own. Windows is unchanged.

> The application's interface is in Italian.

### Changed

- **Buttons answer the click on the Mac.** Pressed, a button shrinks to 95% in 110 ms and springs back on release in 520 ms; the segmented controls (the **Rete** filters, the **Impostazioni** tabs, the theme) slide an orange pill under the choice; the switches, the waiting ring and the day/night fade are drawn by DuckNote itself. They follow the curves of the Windows version, written by hand, instead of the toolkit's ready-made animations.
- **A new Mac icon**: the duck in front of a plain white tile.
- **The disk image opens on a window of its own**: ducks and the skulls of the author's GitHub profile around the edges, and an orange arrow from DuckNote to the **Applicazioni** folder — drag the duck across and it is installed.
