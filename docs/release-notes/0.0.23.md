**In the calendar, a click shows an item; the pencil edits it.**

> The application's interface is in Italian.

### Changed

- **A click on an item opens its card, not the editor**: the day and hour, how often it repeats, its reminders, the host or device it is linked to (with its state, one click opens it) and its notes; things to do and deadlines are marked **Fatto** from the card. The editor opens only from the pencil (or with `E`), and **Annulla** and **Salva** come back to the card.
