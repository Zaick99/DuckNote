**All the room to the sheet.** The formatting bar moved into the title strip, the sidebar slides away with `F9`, and the title and margins scroll away with the text. Under it, a long list of fixes found by driving the application with a simulated keyboard and mouse.

> The application's interface is in Italian.

### Changed

- **The sheet takes the window.** The formatting bar lives in the title strip and the status strip at the bottom is gone: the sheet goes from under the title strip to the bottom edge. The last event appears at the bottom left, stays long enough to be read and leaves by itself.
- **The sidebar hides.** `F9`, `Ctrl+\` or the button next to the traffic lights slide it away and the sheet takes the whole window. While hidden, it peeks over the sheet when the mouse rests on the left edge, and `Ctrl+Shift+F` brings it up with the cursor in the search. The choice is remembered; every passage is animated and can be interrupted halfway.
- **Title and margins scroll with the text.** Two turns of the wheel leave the whole height to what you write. The line you write on keeps air below it, and showing or hiding the sidebar keeps you on the line you were reading. Zooming enlarges the text, not the margins.
- **Saved or not, at a glance.** A dot next to the version is orange while changes are waiting and green when they are on disk. The **Impostazioni** (Settings) gear, pressed again, goes back to the page you were on.

### Fixed

- **Typing fast no longer scrambles the text.** Enter and the shortcuts now wait for the letters still being typed.
- **Addresses written one under the other are all formatted**, pasted ones included; text pasted from a browser or Word comes in as plain text with the note's style.
- **Undo works again.** Colouring an address no longer wipes the undo history, and `Ctrl+Shift+Z` redoes as well as `Ctrl+Y`.
- **The window no longer stops during a scan.** The whole scan runs on worker threads, the ARP table is read in the background with a time limit, and a host that answers is no longer shown as *Errore*.
- **Lists keep their shape at the edges.** Backspace at the start of an item removes the bullet instead of gluing two lines; numbered lists renumber from 1.
- **No formatting the bar cannot show.** The text control's own shortcuts for alignment, font size, indents, superscript and overwrite do nothing; `Ctrl+Shift+L` and `Ctrl+Shift+N` work as in Word.
- **Saving never interrupts writing.** Compressing, encrypting and writing the note happen on another thread.
