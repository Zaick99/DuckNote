**Groups on the left of Rete like the sidebar, with their Ping switch; filters that show the right hosts; cables pulled from port to port.**

> The application's interface is in Italian.

### Added

- **The groups of Rete are a list on the left**, like the items of the sidebar — **Tutti**, **Nota**, your groups, **Ricerca** — with red and green pills and a **Ping** switch each. A click chooses the group: the table, the filters and the search work inside it. The arrow opens a group with its hosts, **Analizza** and **Modifica**.
- **Ping, group by group**: **Tutti** is the whole monitor; **Nota**, each group and **Ricerca** have their own switch.
- **Cables from port to port on the map**: hold a jack, pull, drop on a jack of another device — the cable takes those two ports, named like the device's other ports. A port holds one cable; a taken jack refuses it, and pulling from it moves its cable.

### Changed

- **The card of a host** sits beside the table when there is room, over it otherwise, and closes with **×**.
- **Opening a downloaded update closes DuckNote** (after saving), so the new version can take its place.

### Fixed

- **Tutti, Attivi, Spenti and Da controllare** showed the wrong hosts on the Mac when switching between them.
